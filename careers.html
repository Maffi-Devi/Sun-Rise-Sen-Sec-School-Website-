Careers at Sun Rise

Passionate about teaching? Grow with a school that values its teachers and invests in their development.

Work With Us

Teach, inspire & grow

We welcome qualified and dedicated teachers for PGT, TGT, PRT and NTT positions as vacancies arise. Our teachers enjoy a supportive team, regular training and workshops, and the joy of shaping young lives in a close-knit school community.

  • Regular teacher trainings
  • Supportive leadership
  • Collaborative team
  • Recognition of good work
To apply, send your resume on WhatsApp or call 87088 67177, or email sunrisesr.secschool@yahoo.com.

Documents required for joining

New teachers must submit the following documents:

  1. 1Resume
  2. 2Aadhaar Card
  3. 3PAN Card
  4. 4All education certificates (from Class 10th onwards)
  5. 5Bank passbook copy
  6. 6Experience certificate
  7. 7Family ID
  8. 8CBSE OASIS ID
