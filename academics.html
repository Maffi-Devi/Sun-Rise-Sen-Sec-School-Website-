Academics

A CBSE curriculum from Nursery to Class XII, designed to help children discover themselves and their world through exploration and discovery.

Curriculum

Learning in four stages

Our academic programme follows the CBSE curriculum and the 5+3+3+4 structure of the National Education Policy 2020.

Nursery – Class 2 · Age 3–8

Foundational Stage

Play-based learning with stories, rhymes, colours, toys and activities. Children build language, early numbers, motor skills and good habits in a warm, joyful setting.

Class 3 – 5 · Age 8–11

Preparatory Stage

Reading, writing, speaking, mathematics and environmental studies through activities, projects and discovery — laying a strong base for every subject.

Class 6 – 8 · Age 11–14

Middle Stage

Subject-based learning in Science, Mathematics, Social Science, Languages and Computers, with experiments, projects and exposure to new ideas.

Class 9 – 12 · Age 14–18

Secondary Stage

Focused preparation for CBSE Class X and XII board examinations, with flexible stream choices in Classes XI and XII and career guidance.

Classes XI – XII

Senior secondary streams

Choose the path that matches your interests and career goals.

Arts

For future civil servants, lawyers, teachers and writers.

  • History, Political Science
  • Geography, Economics
  • English, Hindi

Commerce

For careers in business, banking, CA and management.

  • Accountancy
  • Business Studies
  • Economics, English

Non-Medical

For engineering, technology and defence aspirants.

  • Physics, Chemistry
  • Mathematics
  • English

Medical

For future doctors, nurses and life scientists.

  • Physics, Chemistry
  • Biology
  • English

Subject combinations are offered as per CBSE scheme of studies. Please contact the school office for the current list of optional subjects.

Beyond the Classroom

Co-curricular activities

Co-curricular activities are vital for the overall development of a child. We bring out the hidden talent of our students and give them a platform to show it.

House System

Four houses, each led by a House Master / Mistress and house teachers, compete in sports, cultural and academic events.

Excursions & Tours

Educational trips that make students nature lovers and rational thinkers.

Counselling

Expert counselling to help students overcome stress and choose the right course of higher study or career.

Progress Reports & PTM

Performance is shared with parents at the end of every term through progress reports and parent–teacher meetings.

Health Care

Medical help is available on campus to deal with all types of minor ailments.

Remedial Classes

Free remedial classes for students who need extra support — during holidays, assembly time or after school.

Assessment

Regular, fair and transparent evaluation

Students are assessed through four unit tests, a mid-term examination and an annual examination during the year, along with class work, projects and activities.

  • Progress reports shared at the end of each term
  • Parent–Teacher meetings to discuss every child's growth
  • Extra support through free remedial classes
  • Board-exam focused preparation for Classes X and XII
