Campus & Facilities

A safe, green and well-equipped campus where children learn, play and grow with confidence.

Our Campus

Designed for learning & growth

From our colourful pre-primary rooms to senior science labs, every space is planned around the needs of our students.

Academics

Modern Classrooms

Spacious, airy and well-lit classrooms with comfortable desks and green boards.

Digital

Smart Activity Room

A creative space with digital screens for audio-visual learning and group activities.

Labs

Labs

Computer Lab

Library

A quiet reading space with textbooks, reference books and story books for all ages.

Safety First

A safe & certified campus

Your child's safety is our first priority. Our campus holds all mandatory safety certificates, which are renewed from time to time as per norms.

  • Building Safety Certificate (2026-27)
  • Fire Safety Certificate & fire extinguishers on campus
  • Safe Drinking Water & Sanitation Certificate
  • Medical help for minor ailments
  • Strict discipline — no leaving campus during school hours
