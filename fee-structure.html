Fee rules

  • Fee is deposited monthly or quarterly in advance at the school fee counter.
  • Payment accepted by Google Pay, cheque and cash.
  • If fee is not paid on time, the student's name may be struck off the rolls; re-admission is possible on payment of dues and a re-admission charge of ₹500.
  • Transfer Certificate and progress report are issued only after all dues are paid.
