Our Story

Two decades of quality education in Kaithal

Sun Rise Sr. Sec. School was established in 2005 in Village Magho Majri on Khanouri Road, Kaithal, by the Sun Rise Education Society.

What began as a small village school has grown into a full senior secondary school affiliated to the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE), New Delhi, under Affiliation No. 531671 (School No. 41650). Today we teach children from Nursery to Class XII and offer Arts, Commerce and Science streams at the senior secondary level.

Our students come from Magho Majri, Manas, Gadli, Gamri, Kaithal city and many nearby villages. For them, we aim to provide the same quality of education, discipline and exposure that city schools offer — close to home.

From the Principal's Desk

Dear parents & students,

“Education is not only about marks — it is about building character, confidence and a love for learning.”

It gives me great joy to welcome you to Sun Rise Sr. Sec. School. Since 2005, our school has worked with one belief: that children from our villages deserve the very best education, and that with the right guidance, every child can succeed.

Our dedicated teachers focus not only on strong CBSE academics but also on discipline, values, sports and cultural activities. We offer free remedial classes, career counselling and regular parent–teacher meetings, because a child grows best when school and home work together.

I invite you to visit our campus and be a part of the Sun Rise family. I meet parents every day between 10 and 11 AM — my door is always open.

With best wishes,
Khushi Ram, Principal

Four houses, one family

Students are grouped into four houses. Each house is in the charge of a House Master / Mistress assisted by house teachers. Inter-house competitions in sports, debates, quizzes, dance and art build leadership, team spirit and healthy competition.

Every student wears their house T-shirt as part of the school uniform.
