Champions on the field

{{icon:trophy}} State Junior Wushu Championship

Gold and silver medals at Faridabad

At the State Junior Wushu Championship held at Faridabad on 26–27 June, around 450 players from across Haryana took part. Kaithal's players delivered an outstanding performance, winning several gold, silver and bronze medals in different events and weight categories — including players from Sun Rise School.

On their return to Kaithal, the medal winners were welcomed with garlands by the school's director, Khushi Ram Dalal, along with coaches and staff.
