Curriculum

Learning in four stages

Our academic programme follows the CBSE curriculum and the 5+3+3+4 structure of the National Education Policy 2020.

Nursery – Class 2 · Age 3–8

Foundational Stage

Play-based learning with stories, rhymes, colours, toys and activities. Children build language, early numbers, motor skills and good habits in a warm, joyful setting.

Class 3 – 5 · Age 8–11

Preparatory Stage

Reading, writing, speaking, mathematics and environmental studies through activities, projects and discovery — laying a strong base for every subject.

Class 6 – 8 · Age 11–14

Middle Stage

Subject-based learning in Science, Mathematics, Social Science, Languages and Computers, with experiments, projects and exposure to new ideas.

Class 9 – 12 · Age 14–18

Secondary Stage

Focused preparation for CBSE Class X and XII board examinations, with flexible stream choices in Classes XI and XII and career guidance.

Assessment

Students are assessed through four unit tests, a mid-term examination and an annual examination during the year, along with class work, projects and activities.
