This Privacy Policy applies to the website of Sun Rise Sr. Sec. School, Magho Majri (Kaithal), run by Sun Rise Education Society, Mago Majri (“the School”, “we”, “us”). By using this website you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information we collect

You can browse this website without telling us who you are. We collect personal information only when you choose to share it with us, for example when you:

  • fill in the admission enquiry, contact or job application forms;
  • message us on WhatsApp, call us, or send us an email.

This may include the student's name, class applied for and date of birth, the parent's or applicant's name, mobile number, village/city, qualifications and any message you write.

2. How the forms work

Our website does not store form entries on any server or database. When you press Send, the details you typed are placed into a WhatsApp message addressed to the school, which you can review before sending. The message is then handled by WhatsApp under its own privacy policy.

3. How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and guide you through admission.
  • To share information about fees, transport, timings and school events you asked about.
  • To consider job applications for teaching and non-teaching posts.
  • To meet legal and regulatory requirements, including those of CBSE and the Government of Haryana.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to anyone.

4. Students' information and photographs

Student records collected during admission are kept at the school office and used only for school purposes. Photographs of school events and achievements may be shown on this website and our social media pages to celebrate our students. If you would like a photograph of your child removed, please contact the school office and we will remove it promptly.

5. Cookies and third-party services

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies of its own. Some features are provided by third parties who may set their own cookies when you use them:

  • Google Maps loads only when you press Show Map.
  • WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook links open those services, which follow their own privacy policies.

6. Data security and retention

We take reasonable care to protect the information you share with us and keep it only as long as needed for the purpose it was given, or as required by law.

7. Your choices

You may ask us to see, correct or delete the personal information you have shared with us by contacting the school office.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page with the date it was last updated.
