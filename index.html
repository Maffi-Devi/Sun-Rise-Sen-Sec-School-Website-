About Our School

Rise together, shine together

Founded in 2005 by the Sun Rise Education Society, our school has grown into one of the trusted CBSE schools of Kaithal district — serving families from Magho Majri, Manas and more than a dozen surrounding villages.

We believe good education is built on three things: strong academics, sound values and plenty of opportunities to grow beyond textbooks. Our classrooms, activities and house system are designed so every child discovers their strengths through exploration and discovery.

  • Experienced, qualified faculty
  • NEP 2020 aligned learning
  • Free remedial classes
  • Career counselling
  • Four-house system
  • Regular PTMs & progress reports
