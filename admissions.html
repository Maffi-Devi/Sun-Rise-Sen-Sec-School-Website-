Admissions 2026-27

Admissions are open from Nursery to Class XII. Here is everything you need to know to join the Sun Rise family.

How to Apply

Simple admission process

Four easy steps to secure your child's seat for the 2026-27 session.

Enquire & Visit

Call us, send an online enquiry or visit the campus. The prospectus is available at the school office on all working days.

Registration

Fill in the registration form and pay the registration fee for the class applied.

Interaction / Test

No interview for Pre-Nursery & Nursery — only a friendly meeting with parents and child. Other classes: merit-based admission test and interview.

Confirm Admission

Submit the required documents and deposit the fee to confirm your child's admission.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Which classes are admissions open for?

Admissions for session 2026-27 are open from Nursery to Class XII. Class XI admissions are offered in Arts, Commerce and Science streams.

What is the minimum age for Class 1 admission?

As per NEP 2020, a child must be 6 years old as on 31st March of the admission year to join Class 1. Nursery is 3 years, LKG 4 years and UKG 5 years.

Is there an entrance test?

There is no interview for Pre-Nursery and Nursery; only an informal meeting with the parents and child. For other classes, admission is based on merit and is subject to an admission test and interview.

How can fees be paid?

Fees are deposited monthly or quarterly in advance at the school fee counter, by Google Pay, cheque or cash. See the fee structure for class-wise details.

Does the school provide transport?

Yes. School buses cover Magho Majri, Gadli, Gamri, Kaithal and Manas (₹300 per month) and Budha Khera, Atela, Baba Ladana, Sirta, Franswala, Bhanauli, Chika, Carhi and Dharampura (₹400 per month).
